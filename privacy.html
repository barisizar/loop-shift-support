Gizlilik politikası — 1.2.0 ve sonrası

Son güncelleme: 21 Kasım 2026

Loop Shift: Marble Paths, Barış Izar tarafından sunulan bir bulmaca oyunudur. Hesap açman gerekmez. Bu bölüm, reklam içeren 1.2.0 ve sonraki sürümleri kapsar. Reklam içermeyen önceki sürümler (1.1.0 ve 1.0.0) aşağıda ayrıca açıklanır.

Cihazında saklananlar

İlerlemen, yıldızların, oyun içi para, can, ipucu ve takas hakların, sahip olduğun görünümler, günlük hediye ve reklam ödülü sayaçların, öğretici durumun, dil ve oyun tercihlerin yalnızca cihazında saklanır. Bunlar bir oyuncu hesabıyla sunucuya eşitlenmez ve geliştiriciye gönderilmez. Uygulamayı silmek yerel kayıtları kaldırabilir. Cihaz yedekleri Apple'ın hizmetlerine ve senin ayarlarına tabidir.

Reklamlar

1.2.0 sürümünden itibaren oyun Google AdMob reklamları gösterir. Ödüllü reklamlar isteğe bağlıdır: yalnızca sen izleme düğmesine bastığında gösterilir ve oyun içi para, can veya ek ipucu verir. Ödül, sağlayıcının tamamlanma bildiriminden sonra eklenir. Ayrıca tamamlanan bazı bölümlerin arasında, sıklık ve günlük sayı sınırlarıyla, ödülsüz tam ekran reklam gösterilebilir. Bulmaca sırasında reklam gösterilmez. Reklamlar önceden hazırlanabilir.

Google'ın reklam SDK'sı reklam sunmak, reklamları ölçmek, gösterim sıklığını sınırlamak ve dolandırıcılığı önlemek için şunları işleyebilir: IP adresi ve bundan türetilen yaklaşık konum; cihaz ve uygulama tanımlayıcıları; gösterilen reklamlar ve reklam etkileşimleri; uygulama içi etkileşimler; performans, çökme ve diğer tanılama verileri. Bu veriler Google tarafından kendi politikalarına göre işlenir ve başka ülkelerde işlenebilir. Google Mobile Ads veri açıklaması · Google'ın iş ortağı uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri kullanımı.

Takip izni

Oyun ilk açılışta Apple'ın takip izni penceresini gösterir. İzin verirsen cihazının reklam tanımlayıcısı (IDFA) reklam sağlayıcısının kullanımına açılır. Bu tanımlayıcı, sana daha ilgili (kişiselleştirilmiş) reklamlar göstermek ve reklam performansını ölçmek için başka şirketlerin uygulamalarından ve sitelerinden elde edilen verilerle ilişkilendirilebilir. İzin vermezsen reklam tanımlayıcısı kullanılamaz ve oyun bütün reklam isteklerini kişiselleştirilmemiş olarak gönderir.

Kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlar da bağlama ve yaklaşık bölgeye göre seçilebilir; gösterim sıklığını sınırlama, toplu ölçüm ve dolandırıcılığı önleme için sınırlı veri işlemeyi gerektirir. Seçimin oyunun hiçbir özelliğini değiştirmez. Tercihini istediğin zaman iOS Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → Takip bölümünden değiştirebilirsin. Kişiselleştirilmiş ve kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlar.

Bölgesel izin ve tercihler

Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de ilk reklam isteğinden önce Google'ın izin formu gösterilir; formda izin verebilir veya seçeneklerini yönetebilirsin. Takip izni penceresi bu formdan sonra gösterilir ve formda verdiğin karar her zaman geçerlidir. ABD'nin ilgili eyaletlerinde reklam gizlilik tercihleri sunulur. Seçenekler mevcut olduğunda Ayarlar'daki “Reklam gizlilik tercihleri” bağlantısıyla formu yeniden açabilirsin. Reklam içerik sınırı genel izleyici (G) olarak ayarlanmıştır.

Analiz

1.2.0 sürümü Firebase veya başka bir kullanım analizi hizmeti içermez ve oyun verilerini geliştiriciye göndermez. Reklam sağlayıcısının kendi ölçümleri yukarıda açıklanmıştır.

Çocuklar

Oyun genel izleyiciye yöneliktir; özellikle çocuklara yönelik değildir. Geliştirici çocuklardan bilerek kişisel veri toplamaz. iOS çocuk hesaplarında takip iznini kapalı tutar; ebeveynler Ekran Süresi ayarlarıyla uygulamaların takip izni istemesini engelleyebilir.

Privacy policy — 1.2.0 and later

Updated November 21, 2026

Loop Shift: Marble Paths is a puzzle game provided by Barış Izar. No player account is required. This section covers version 1.2.0 and later, which contain advertising. The earlier ad-free versions (1.1.0 and 1.0.0) are described separately below.

Stored on your device

Progress, stars, coins, lives, hints and swap tokens, owned styles, daily gift and ad reward counters, tutorial state, language and preferences are stored only on your device. They are not synchronized to a server through a player account and are not sent to the developer. Removing the app may remove these records. Device backups follow Apple's services and your settings.

Advertising

From version 1.2.0 the game shows Google AdMob ads. Rewarded ads are optional: they appear only after you select a watch button and give coins, lives or an extra hint. The reward follows the provider's earned-reward callback. Non-rewarded full-screen ads may also appear between some completed levels, subject to frequency and daily limits. No ad is shown during a puzzle. Ads may be prepared in advance.

To deliver and measure ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK may process: IP address and the approximate location derived from it; device and app identifiers; ads shown and ad interactions; in-app interactions; and performance, crash and other diagnostic data. Google processes this data under its own policies and may do so in other countries. Google Mobile Ads data disclosure · How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Tracking permission

On first launch the game shows Apple's tracking prompt. If you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) becomes available to the advertising provider. It may be linked with data from other companies' apps and websites to show you more relevant (personalized) ads and to measure ad performance. If you do not allow it, the advertising identifier is unavailable and the game sends every ad request as non-personalized.

Non-personalized ads may still be selected by context and approximate region, and require limited data processing for frequency limits, aggregate measurement and fraud prevention. Your choice does not change any feature of the game. You can change it at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Personalized and non-personalized ads.

Regional consent and choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before the first ad request; you can consent or manage your options there. The tracking prompt appears after this form, and the decision you make in the form always applies. In the relevant US states, advertising privacy choices are offered. When options are available, you can reopen the form through “Ad privacy choices” in Settings. The maximum ad content rating is set to general audience (G).

Analytics

Version 1.2.0 does not include Firebase or any other usage analytics service and does not send gameplay data to the developer. The advertising provider's own measurement is described above.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. iOS keeps tracking permission off for child accounts, and parents can prevent apps from asking to track in Screen Time settings.

Contact and external pages

Support, access or deletion requests: Barış Izar · barisizar@gmail.com. Your email address and chosen message content are used to respond; please avoid unnecessary personal information. The game has no access to the data processed by the advertising provider; you can also exercise your rights over that data through Google's own tools.

Support and privacy links open in your browser. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; the host may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, for service operation and security. We add no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to these pages. GitHub privacy statement.

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before that version becomes available.

Privacy disclosures for 1.0.1 test builds — English

Version scope clarified November 3, 2026; the data-practice disclosures below are retained from the October 19, 2026 notice.

Loop Shift: Marble Paths is a puzzle game provided by Barış Izar. No player account is required. This section covers the 1.0.1 iPhone test builds with rewarded and between-level advertising and optional usage analytics; it does not apply to the App Store versions (1.0.0, 1.1.0, 1.2.0).

Game saves and optional analytics

Progress, stars, virtual coin/life/booster balances, tutorial state and preferences are saved on your device without player-account server synchronization. Removing the app may remove these records. Device backups follow Apple's services and your settings.

Usage analytics is off by default. If you agree, Google Analytics for Firebase measures level starts, outcomes and duration; tutorials and hints; shop previews, coin purchases and equipped styles; rewards and virtual balance changes. Ad loading, display, clicks, rewards and impression revenue amounts/currencies may also be measured. Spending virtual coins is not a real-money purchase.

Technical measurements may include app/device characteristics, approximate region, timestamps and sessions, sampled frame times and memory use. The game's error diagnostics use a bounded number of hashed error identifiers; our own analytics events do not send full error messages, stack traces or crash dumps. Events are associated with a random, pseudonymous installation/app-instance identifier generated by Firebase. This identifier does not contain your name, but it connects events from the same installation, so we do not describe this data as anonymous. Reinstallation or an analytics reset may change it. We do not add names, emails, phone numbers or advertising identifiers to our own analytics events.

Processing, retention and your choices

Consented analytics data is processed in the developer's Google Analytics project. Daily export to Google BigQuery is enabled for detailed gameplay analysis, with its export dataset location configured as the European Union (EU) and advertising-identifier export disabled. The current BigQuery Sandbox configuration applies automatic 60-day table expiration. This 60-day period applies only to the current Sandbox tables; it does not mean every Google record is deleted after 60 days. Google Analytics and advertising service records follow their own retention settings and service policies. The EU setting is the BigQuery export dataset location; it does not mean all Google processing occurs in the EU. Service providers may also process data in other countries. We will update this notice if the configuration changes. Firebase privacy information · BigQuery Sandbox information.

You can turn analytics off in Settings. Subsequent analytics collection stops, the game's pending local analytics/diagnostic records are cleared, and local Firebase analytics state is reset. This does not automatically delete Google Analytics/BigQuery records already sent. Use the contact below for access or deletion requests. Analytics consent does not grant ad-personalization or cross-app tracking permission. Turning analytics off does not disable the separate technical processing used by the advertising provider to operate ads.

Rewarded and between-level ads

You may choose to watch an AdMob ad for virtual coins, lives or an extra hint; rewards follow the provider's earned-reward callback. Rewarded ads appear after you select their watch button. Non-rewarded full-screen ads may also appear at transitions between completed levels, subject to frequency and daily limits. Ads may be prepared in advance. Google's ad SDK may process IP addresses and derived approximate location; device/app identifiers; ads shown and ad interactions; product interactions; and performance, crash and other diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement and security. This processing is separate from optional game analytics. Although our own analytics does not send crash dumps, the ad SDK may collect crash data. Lack of IDFA permission does not mean all device/app identifiers or this technical data are absent. Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Google's advertising privacy form appears when required; the app checks whether it may request ads first. When options are available, you can reopen them through the ad privacy link in Settings. These choices are separate from usage analytics. The app does not request Apple's ATT permission for IDFA access.

For 1.0.1 (53) and subsequent 1.0.1 test builds, ad requests are configured for non-personalized ads, with publisher first-party ID and ad personalization disabled. The maximum ad content rating is set to general audience (G). These settings are not remotely applied to the earlier test build 52. Non-personalized ads may still use context and approximate location and require limited data processing for frequency limits, aggregate measurement and fraud prevention. These settings do not replace consent forms where consent is required. Personalized and non-personalized ads.
